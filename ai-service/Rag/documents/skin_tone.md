# Men's Skin Tones

## Fair / Light
- Burns easily, may have freckles or visible veins.
- Undertones: Usually cool or neutral.
- Best clothing colors: Navy, gray, soft blues, burgundy, emerald.
- Makeup/grooming note: Choose foundations and concealers carefully if used.

## Light-Medium
- Sometimes tans, sometimes burns.
- Very common skin tone.
- Flexible with most colors.

## Medium / Olive
- Tans easily, rarely burns.
- Often has green or golden undertones.
- Best colors: Earth tones, white, black, jewel tones, olive, rust.

## Tan / Medium-Deep
- Naturally golden or light brown.
- Looks good in both warm and cool colors.
- Strong with saturated colors and contrast.

## Deep / Dark
- Rich brown to deep ebony.
- Best colors: Bright, bold, and high-contrast colors. White, cream, and metallics also look excellent.
- Avoid: Very muted or ashy tones that lack contrast.

## Undertones Explained
- Cool: Pink, red, or bluish.
- Warm: Golden, peachy, or yellow.
- Neutral: Mix of both.

## How to Identify Your Undertone
- Look at the veins on your wrist:
  - Blue/purple → cool
  - Green → warm
  - Both → neutral
- Jewelry test: Silver flatters cool, gold flatters warm.

## Why Skin Tone Matters for Style
- Right colors make you look healthier and more put-together.
- Wrong colors can make you look tired or washed out.
