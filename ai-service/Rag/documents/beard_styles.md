# Beard Styles Guide

## Classic Full Beard
- Full coverage of cheeks, chin, and jawline.
- Best for: Oval, rectangular, and square face shapes.
- Maintenance: Trim every 1–2 weeks, oil daily.
- Length: 1–3 inches for a polished look.

## Stubble / 5 o'clock Shadow
- Short, even growth (1–3 mm).
- Best for: Most face shapes, especially round and oval.
- Maintenance: Use a stubble trimmer every 2–3 days.
- Gives a rugged yet clean appearance.

## Goatee
- Hair on the chin only, sometimes with a mustache.
- Best for: Long or rectangular faces (adds width to the chin).
- Avoid if: Very round face (can make it look longer).

## Van Dyke
- Goatee + disconnected mustache.
- Best for: Square and diamond faces.
- Style tip: Keep the mustache slightly longer than the chin hair.

## Circle Beard
- Mustache connected to a rounded goatee (no cheek hair).
- Best for: Oval and heart-shaped faces.
- Clean and professional look.

## Mutton Chops
- Thick sideburns that extend toward the chin, no chin connection.
- Best for: Strong jawlines and rectangular faces.
- High-maintenance style.

## Beard Length Guide
- Short (0.5–1 inch): Professional, low maintenance.
- Medium (1–2 inches): Most versatile.
- Long (3+ inches): Statement style, requires more care and shaping.

## General Tips
- Match beard style to face shape for balance.
- Use beard oil and balm to keep hair soft and skin healthy.
- Always define the neckline (two fingers above the Adam’s apple).
