# Men's Body Types

## Ectomorph
- Naturally thin, narrow shoulders and hips, low body fat.
- Hard to gain weight or muscle.
- Clothing tips: Layering, structured jackets, horizontal patterns to add width.
- Avoid: Oversized baggy clothes that make you look thinner.

## Mesomorph
- Athletic build, broad shoulders, narrow waist, gains muscle easily.
- Balanced proportions.
- Clothing tips: Most styles work well. Fitted shirts, tailored pants, V-necks.
- Strength: Can pull off both slim and slightly relaxed fits.

## Endomorph
- Softer, rounder build, wider hips and waist, gains fat easily.
- Clothing tips: Dark colors, vertical stripes, structured shoulders, higher-rise pants.
- Avoid: Tight shirts that cling to the midsection and low-rise pants.

## Inverted Triangle
- Broad shoulders and chest, narrower waist and hips.
- Common in athletes (swimmers, weightlifters).
- Clothing tips: Balanced silhouettes, avoid overly shoulder-padded jackets.

## Rectangle / Straight
- Shoulders, waist, and hips are similar in width.
- Clothing tips: Create shape with layering, belts, and structured outerwear.

## General Styling Rules by Body Type
- Emphasize your best features.
- Create the illusion of an inverted triangle (broad shoulders, narrower waist) when possible.
- Fit is more important than brand or trend.
