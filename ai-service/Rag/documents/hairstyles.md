# Men's Hairstyles

## Short Styles
- Buzz Cut: Uniform short length. Low maintenance. Works on most face shapes.
- Crew Cut: Slightly longer on top, faded sides.
- Ivy League: Longer version of the crew cut, can be parted.
- Textured Crop: Short sides, textured and slightly messy top. Very modern.

## Medium Length Styles
- Side Part: Classic and professional. Works with most face shapes.
- Quiff: Volume on top, swept upward and back.
- Pompadour: Higher and more dramatic version of the quiff.
- French Crop: Short fringe, textured top, faded or tapered sides.

## Longer Styles
- Medium Length Flow: Natural, slightly wavy or straight, falls around the ears and neck.
- Man Bun / Top Knot: Practical for longer hair.
- Curtains: Middle part with hair falling on both sides of the face.

## Styles by Face Shape (Quick Reference)
- Oval: Almost anything.
- Round: Height on top, short sides.
- Square: Textured top or soft layers.
- Rectangular: Avoid too much height; add width.
- Heart: Volume near the chin or side-swept styles.

## Maintenance Tips
- Get a haircut every 3–6 weeks depending on style.
- Use the right product: matte clay for texture, pomade for shine and hold, sea salt spray for natural look.
- Always consider hair density and texture (straight, wavy, curly) when choosing a style.
