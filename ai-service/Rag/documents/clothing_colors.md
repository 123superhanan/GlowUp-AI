# Clothing Colors for Men

## Neutral Base Colors
- Navy, charcoal, gray, black, olive, beige, white, cream.
- These form the foundation of a versatile wardrobe.
- Easy to mix and match.

## Best Colors by Skin Tone

### Fair / Cool Skin
- Best: Navy, charcoal, emerald, burgundy, soft pastels.
- Avoid: Orange, bright yellow, neon colors.

### Medium / Olive Skin
- Best: Almost all colors. Earth tones, jewel tones, white, and black work especially well.
- Strong choices: Forest green, rust, deep blue, burgundy.

### Dark / Deep Skin
- Best: Bright and saturated colors, white, cream, gold, bold reds and blues.
- Avoid: Muddy or overly muted tones that can wash you out.

## Color Combinations That Work
- Navy + white + light gray
- Olive + cream + brown
- Charcoal + burgundy + white
- Black + any bright accent color

## Seasonal Color Tips
- Spring/Summer: Lighter neutrals, pastels, soft blues and greens.
- Fall/Winter: Deeper tones — burgundy, forest green, camel, charcoal.

## Rules of Thumb
- Limit bright colors to one statement piece per outfit.
- Neutrals make the outfit look more expensive and intentional.
