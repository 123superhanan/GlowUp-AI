# Common Skin Conditions in Men

## Acne
- Caused by excess oil, clogged pores, bacteria, and hormones.
- Care: Gentle cleanser twice daily, non-comedogenic moisturizer, avoid heavy oils on the face.
- For beards: Keep the area clean and use lightweight beard oil.

## Dry Skin
- Flaky, tight, sometimes itchy.
- Care: Hydrating cleanser, richer moisturizer, avoid hot showers.
- Look for ingredients: hyaluronic acid, ceramides, glycerin.

## Oily Skin
- Shiny appearance, enlarged pores, prone to breakouts.
- Care: Gel-based or foaming cleansers, lightweight moisturizers, blotting papers.
- Do not skip moisturizer — it can make oil production worse.

## Combination Skin
- Oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin), normal or dry cheeks.
- Care: Balance is key. Use gentle products and moisturize where needed.

## Sensitive Skin
- Easily irritated, redness, stinging.
- Care: Fragrance-free products, minimal ingredients, patch test new products.
- Avoid alcohol-based toners and harsh scrubs.

## Razor Burn & Ingrown Hairs
- Common with shaving.
- Prevention: Proper prep (warm water + shaving cream), sharp razor, shave with the grain, use aftershave balm.
- For beards: Exfoliate regularly and keep the skin underneath clean.

## General Skincare Routine for Men
1. Cleanse
2. Treat (optional serums)
3. Moisturize
4. Sunscreen every morning (SPF 30+)
