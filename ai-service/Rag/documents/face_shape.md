# Men's Face Shapes

## Oval
- Balanced proportions, slightly longer than wide, rounded jaw.
- Most versatile face shape.
- Almost any hairstyle and beard works.

## Round
- Soft jawline, similar width and length.
- Goal: Add length and angles.
- Best styles: Longer hair on top, short sides, angular beards, side parts.
- Avoid: Full round beards and very short all-over cuts.

## Square
- Strong jaw, wide forehead, angular features.
- Goal: Soften the angles or emphasize them intentionally.
- Best styles: Textured crops, longer top with faded sides, full beards or clean-shaven.

## Rectangular / Oblong
- Longer face, high forehead, straight cheeks.
- Goal: Add width.
- Best styles: Medium length hair with volume on the sides, beards that add width (full or circle beards).
- Avoid: Very long hair or tall styles that add more height.

## Heart / Inverted Triangle
- Wider forehead, narrower chin.
- Goal: Balance the upper and lower face.
- Best styles: Medium length with some volume at the chin area, goatees, side-swept bangs.

## Diamond
- Narrow forehead and chin, wide cheekbones.
- Goal: Soften the cheekbones and add width to forehead/chin.
- Best styles: Textured styles with some height, beards that fill the chin area.

## How to Determine Your Face Shape
1. Pull hair back.
2. Look in the mirror or take a photo.
3. Measure forehead, cheekbones, jawline, and face length.
4. Compare the measurements.
